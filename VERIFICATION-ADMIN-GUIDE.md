# Commissioner verification rules

## Creator

Verify each claim separately:

1. Identity
2. Account ownership
3. Followers
4. Engagement

Do not approve a follower number simply because the creator typed it into their profile.

Until an authorized platform integration is available, mark follower/engagement claims as unverified.

## Business

Verify each claim separately:

1. Registration
2. License
3. Authorized representative

A valid registration/license means the supplied business information was checked. It is **not** a guarantee that every transaction or interaction with the business will be good.

## Public language

Prefer:

- “Followers checked”
- “License checked”
- “Identity checked”
- “Last checked …”

Avoid:

- “100% safe”
- “Guaranteed trustworthy”
- “Scam-proof”
- “Commissioner guarantees this seller”

## Rechecks

Verification records have `checked_at` and `expires_at`. Recheck information that can change, especially creator statistics and business licenses.
